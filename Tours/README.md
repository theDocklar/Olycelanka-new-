# Curated Sri Lanka tours

Five proposed, customisable packages used by the existing tour listing, detail and enquiry pages. Edit `packages.json` to update content. Images reuse the project’s existing local photo assets; confirm usage rights before commercial publication. Cover images illustrate the itinerary rather than guaranteed experiences.

Prices are sample planning estimates in USD per person for two adults sharing, not supplier quotes. Availability, accommodation, transport, train seats, park access and final inclusions must be confirmed before sale. Wildlife sightings and sea conditions cannot be guaranteed.

Destination reference: https://www.srilanka.travel/top-attractions and https://www.srilanka.travel/cultural_heritage
